Cycle Detection in Signature Images

Author: Zulfiqar Ali Khan
Journal: International Journal of Scientific & Engineering Research (IJSER)
Volume: 4
Issue: 11
Publication Date: November 2013

About the Paper

This research investigates cycle detection in signature images and explores computational techniques for identifying cyclic structures within signature image data.

Developing Students' Entrepreneurial Skills Through E-TShirt Project

Authors: Zulfiqar Ali Khan and Dr. Raheel Siddiqui
Journal: Global Education Journal
Volume: 2012
Issue: 1
Pages: 158–168
Publication Year: 2012
Publisher: Franklin Publishing

About the Paper

This research examines how engineering students can develop entrepreneurial and business skills through a project-based learning activity involving the design, development, exhibition, and sale of electronic T-shirts (e-TShirts). The study also evaluates students' learning experiences and the development of entrepreneurial skills through the project.

Citation
Khan, Z. A., & Siddiqui, R. (2012). Developing Students' Entrepreneurial Skills Through E-TShirt Project. Global Education Journal, 2012(1), 158–168.
